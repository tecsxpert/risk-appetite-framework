Operational Risk Appetite Framework

Operational risk is the risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, systems, or external events. It includes fraud risk, technology risk, and compliance risk.

Operational risk appetite components:
- Zero tolerance for fraud and misconduct
- Maximum acceptable system downtime: 4 hours per year
- Maximum acceptable data breach incidents: zero tolerance
- Staff error rate threshold: less than 0.1% of transactions

Key operational risk indicators:
- Number of failed transactions per day
- System availability percentage
- Staff turnover rate in key roles
- Number of regulatory breaches
- Customer complaint volumes

Mitigation strategies include robust internal controls, regular staff training, business continuity planning, and comprehensive insurance coverage.
