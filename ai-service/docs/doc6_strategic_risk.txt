Strategic Risk Appetite Framework

Strategic risk refers to the risk of loss arising from adverse business decisions, improper implementation of decisions, or lack of responsiveness to industry changes.

Strategic risk appetite components:
- Maximum acceptable revenue concentration: 30% from single product
- Minimum acceptable return on equity: 12%
- Maximum acceptable cost-to-income ratio: 55%
- New market entry risk tolerance: moderate

Strategic risk indicators:
- Market share trends
- Customer acquisition and retention rates
- Product profitability metrics
- Competitor analysis results

Organizations should conduct annual strategic risk reviews aligned with business planning cycles.
