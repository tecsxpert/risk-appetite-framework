Compliance Risk Appetite Framework

Compliance risk is the risk of legal or regulatory sanctions, financial loss, or reputational damage resulting from failure to comply with laws, regulations, or internal policies.

Compliance risk appetite:
- Zero tolerance for regulatory breaches
- Zero tolerance for anti-money laundering violations
- Maximum acceptable compliance findings per audit: 5 minor findings
- Mandatory staff compliance training completion: 100%

Key compliance requirements:
- Anti-Money Laundering regulations
- Know Your Customer requirements
- Data protection regulations (GDPR)
- Capital adequacy requirements (Basel III)

Compliance monitoring includes regular internal audits, regulatory reporting, staff training programs, and independent compliance reviews.
