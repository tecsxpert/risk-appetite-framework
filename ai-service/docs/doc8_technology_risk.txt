Technology Risk Appetite Framework

Technology risk includes risks related to IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, data integrity, and technology-driven business disruption.

Technology risk appetite:
- Zero tolerance for critical data loss
- Maximum acceptable cybersecurity incidents per year: zero critical incidents
- System availability requirement: 99.9% uptime
- Maximum acceptable recovery time objective: 4 hours

Key technology risks:
- Cybersecurity attacks including ransomware and phishing
- System failures and outages
- Data breaches and unauthorized access
- Technology obsolescence

Controls include multi-factor authentication, regular penetration testing, disaster recovery planning, and continuous security monitoring.
