Risk Governance Framework

Risk governance refers to the structures, processes, and mechanisms that ensure risks are managed effectively across an organization.

Key governance components:
- Board Risk Committee: oversight of overall risk appetite
- Chief Risk Officer: responsible for enterprise risk management
- Risk Management Committee: operational risk oversight
- Three Lines of Defence model implementation

Risk appetite statement requirements:
- Quantitative metrics for each risk category
- Clear ownership and accountability
- Regular review and board approval
- Alignment with strategic objectives

Effective risk governance includes clear escalation procedures, transparent risk reporting, and integration of risk management into business decision-making.
