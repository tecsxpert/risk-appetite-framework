Market Risk Appetite Framework

Market risk refers to the risk of losses in positions arising from movements in market prices. This includes equity risk, interest rate risk, currency risk, and commodity risk.

Market risk appetite should define:
- Value at Risk (VaR) limits: maximum acceptable daily loss at 95% confidence
- Stop-loss limits: automatic position closure at predefined loss levels
- Concentration limits: maximum exposure to single asset class
- Liquidity requirements: minimum percentage of liquid assets

Key market risk metrics:
- VaR: measures potential loss over specific time horizon
- Expected Shortfall: average loss beyond VaR threshold
- Stress Test Results: losses under extreme market scenarios
- Beta: sensitivity of portfolio to market movements

Organizations should review market risk appetite quarterly and adjust limits based on market conditions and organizational risk tolerance.
