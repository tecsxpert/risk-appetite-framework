Liquidity Risk Appetite Framework

Liquidity risk is the risk that an organization cannot meet its financial obligations as they come due without incurring unacceptable losses. Managing liquidity risk is critical for financial stability.

Liquidity risk appetite should specify:
- Minimum liquidity coverage ratio: 100% as per Basel III requirements
- Minimum net stable funding ratio: 100%
- Stress scenario survival period: minimum 30 days
- Maximum reliance on short-term funding: 20% of total funding

Liquidity risk monitoring:
- Daily cash flow forecasting
- Weekly liquidity stress testing
- Monthly funding gap analysis
- Quarterly review of liquidity contingency plan

Early warning indicators include rapid deposit outflows, inability to roll over wholesale funding, and credit rating downgrades.
