Risk appetite is the level of risk that an organization is prepared to accept in pursuit of its objectives. 
A Risk Appetite Framework (RAF) includes the policies, processes, and systems through which risk appetite is established, communicated, and monitored. 

Key elements of a robust RAF:
- Risk Appetite Statement: An articulation of the aggregate level and types of risk the firm is willing to take.
- Risk Limits: Quantitative measures based on forward-looking assumptions.
- Roles and Responsibilities: Clear accountability for management and the board.

Organizations must maintain a balance between risk and reward to ensure long-term sustainability and growth.
