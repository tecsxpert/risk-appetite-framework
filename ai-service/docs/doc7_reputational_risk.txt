Reputational Risk Appetite Framework

Reputational risk is the risk of loss arising from negative public opinion, which may impair an organization's ability to retain and attract customers, staff, or business partners.

Reputational risk appetite:
- Zero tolerance for ethical violations
- Maximum acceptable negative media coverage: monitored weekly
- Customer satisfaction score minimum: 75%
- Employee engagement score minimum: 70%

Reputational risk triggers:
- Data breaches affecting customer data
- Regulatory enforcement actions
- Senior management misconduct
- Product failures causing customer harm

Mitigation strategies include strong corporate governance, transparent communication, proactive stakeholder engagement, and robust crisis management plans.
